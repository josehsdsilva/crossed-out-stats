Privacy Policy

Crossed Out Stats · last updated 18 November 2026

What this app is

Crossed Out Stats is a private tool used only by the team behind the Crossed Out channel. It is not offered to the public and only the channel owner signs in to it.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (Google sign-in, read-only scopes youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly): our own channel's subscriber count and per-video statistics such as views, average view duration and subscribers gained.
  • TikTok, Instagram and Facebook (read-only): view, like, comment and share counts, watch time and follower counts of our own accounts.

The app does not read data about viewers, other users or other channels, and it never posts, edits or deletes content.

How the data is used and stored

The statistics are saved on a private server owned by the channel team and shown on an internal dashboard that is not reachable from the public internet. They are used only to decide which kinds of stories to make next. The data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Access tokens are stored only on that private server and are used solely to request the statistics listed above.

Google API Services

Crossed Out Stats' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or from the security settings of the other platforms. Stored statistics can be deleted on request.
