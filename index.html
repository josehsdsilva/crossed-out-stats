Crossed Out Stats

Crossed Out Stats is a private, internal tool used by the team behind the Crossed Out short-video channel (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook: @crossedoutstories).

It reads the view, like, comment and watch-time statistics of our own channel and accounts, and shows them on a private dashboard so we can see which stories work best. It does not post, edit or delete anything, it is not offered to other people, and it does not access anyone else's data.

Read more in the privacy policy.
